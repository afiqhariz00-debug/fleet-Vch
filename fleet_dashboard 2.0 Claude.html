Key Insights

🏆
Highest Mileage Vehicles
PNB 118 (Perodua Aruz) and PNB 111 (Toyota Innova Zenix) are the most-used vehicles at 14,581 and 14,537 km respectively — together accounting for 18% of total fleet mileage.
⚡
Best Fuel Efficiency
PNB 90 and PNB 200 (Perodua Alza SE) are the most fuel-efficient at ~RM 0.06/km. PNB 400 and WC 1191 H (Ford Ranger) are next at ~RM 0.13–0.14/km.
⚠️
High Cost-per-KM Vehicles
PNB 100 (RM 0.37/km), PNB 900 (RM 0.34/km), and PNB 600 (RM 0.31/km) have the highest petrol cost per km — potentially indicating inefficiency or heavy engines.
📈
Peak Usage Months
July (27,668 km) and August (22,563 km) 2025 saw the highest fleet utilisation. October recorded the highest petrol spend (RM 4,245), likely driven by increased operational demands.
🛣️
Touch 'n Go Outlier
PNB 200 and PNB 100 have disproportionately high Touch 'n Go costs (RM 2,030 and RM 830) vs low petrol costs — suggesting highway-heavy routes with minimal distance.
🚌
Toyota Hiace: Cost Leader
PNB 700 (Toyota Hiace) has the highest total fleet cost at RM 4,129/year, driven by high TnG usage (RM 930). The Hiace fleet (PNB 600 + 700) costs RM 5,545 combined annually.
